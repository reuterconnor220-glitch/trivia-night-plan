01 — Overview

The Quick Rundown

Caruso Ventures hosts a casual trivia night during Boulder Startup Week for young professionals (~30 and under, or generally early in their careers) — founders, operators, and investors who are just getting started. Drinks are on us. Trivia keeps the energy up and makes sure nobody stands in a corner.

4 rounds of trivia (General Knowledge, VC & Startups, Boulder & Colorado, Wild Card), a trivia host sourced from our network, and a $1,000 budget that goes almost entirely to a bar tab. Teams are randomized at the door so everyone meets someone new.

02 — Why We're Doing This

The Goals

01

Brand Positioning

Put Caruso Ventures on the map with the next generation of Boulder's builders and investors — before they're running big funds or scaling companies.

02

Community Building

Create a repeatable, signature event. Year 1 is the foundation — "Caruso Trivia Night" should become something people circle on their calendar every BSW.

03

Relationships & Deal Flow

Every person in the room is a potential co-investor, deal source, future founder, or hire. The trivia format forces real connections, not just business card swaps.

03 — Marketing & Promotion

How We Fill the Room

Target audience: young professionals roughly 30 and under, or generally early in their careers — founders, operators, VCs, analysts, emerging fund managers.

Our Channels

  • Personal outreach: Connor and Gib text, DM, and email the people they want in the room
  • Sean from Endeavor: Loop him in early to co-promote and bring his network
  • LinkedIn: Post from Caruso Ventures page + personal accounts. Make it shareable.
  • Slack: Post in #boulderstartups, Colorado VC channels, founder groups
  • Custom registration website: Built by our team for RSVPs. Cap at 60, then waitlist.

Getting on BSW's Calendar

  • Submit via boulderstartupweek.com: BSW has an open submission process. Anyone can host an event — submit event name, description, date, time, and venue.
  • Timeline: Submit ASAP once venue is locked. Listings go live ~3-4 weeks before BSW.
  • Why it matters: BSW's calendar gets 5,000+ views during the week. Free exposure to exactly the audience we want. Our event shows up alongside panels, workshops, and happy hours.
  • Link to our registration site: The BSW listing can point directly to our custom RSVP page.
